Olivier Cauquil

After more than twenty-five years as a French–English translator and voice-over artist, I have stepped back from translation work and am no longer taking on new projects.

My thanks to the clients and colleagues I have worked with over the years, from football clubs and broadcasters to businesses large and small. It has been a privilege.

Après plus de vingt-cinq ans comme traducteur et voix off français–anglais, j’ai mis fin à mon activité de traduction et n’accepte plus de nouveaux projets. Merci à tous ceux avec qui j’ai eu le plaisir de travailler.

These days you can find me on Etsy.

I can still be reached at arvouille@cauquil.co.uk.
